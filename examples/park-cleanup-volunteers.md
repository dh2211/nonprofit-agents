## Team leader role (`npo-volunteer-role`)

**Cleanup team leader**

**Purpose.** Lead a small team to clean one area of Millbrook Park safely, so the park is better for everyone who uses it.

**Tasks**

- Attend a 30-minute briefing on 2 May.
- Arrive at 8:15 to set up.
- Lead your team to its area and give out equipment.
- Remind your team of the safety rules, and mark and report any sharps.
- Keep under-16s with their adult.
- Bring bags to the collection point at 11:45.

**Time.** Saturday 9 May, 8:15 to 12:15, plus the briefing.
