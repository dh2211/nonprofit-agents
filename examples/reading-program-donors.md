# Youth reading program: year-end donors

> **Request.** We're Open Page Reading Club (invented), an after-school reading program. Write a short year-end impact update for individual donors, a thank-you letter for a first-time monthly donor, and a year-end appeal email. Our numbers (program records, school year 2025 to 2026): 96 children enrolled; 1,450 tutoring sessions; 38 volunteer tutors. Reading assessment (same test, September and May): 61 of the 88 children who took both tests moved up at least one reading level. One story, with written consent from a parent, first name only: "Sam, 9, read his first chapter book aloud to his little sister in March." What didn't go well: we had a waiting list of 20 children all year because we lacked tutors. 40 buys one child a month of weekly sessions (our cost figure). Executive director signs as "Lena". Appeal goal: 12,000 by 31 December.

## Impact update for donors (`npo-impact-report`)

**What your support did this school year (2025 to 2026)**

This year, 96 children came to Open Page Reading Club after school, and 38 volunteer tutors sat with them for 1,450 sessions.

We test reading levels in September and May. Of the 88 children who took both tests, 61 moved up at least one reading level.

In March, Sam, 9, read his first chapter book aloud to his little sister.

Not everything went to plan. Twenty children waited for a place all year because we didn't have enough tutors. This year we're recruiting tutors earlier, in August.

Thank you. None of this happens without you.
