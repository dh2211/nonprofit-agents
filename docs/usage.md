# Use the skills

After you [install the skills](install.md), ask your agent for what you need in plain English. The agent matches your request to a skill's description and loads it. You can also name the skill: "Use the npo-impact-report skill to..."

## Before you start

- Check that your organization allows the AI tool you're using, and for which data.
- For any grant, read the funder's guidelines and their policy on AI-assisted applications. Some restrict AI use or ask you to disclose it.

## Brief it like a colleague

The skills ask for missing details, but a complete first request saves a round trip. Share real facts:

- your mission and the program,
- numbers from your records, with dates and sources,
- the funder's guidelines pasted in, with word limits,
- your tone and who signs it.

Leave out donor, volunteer, and client names and contact details. Use placeholders such as `[donor name]`.

## Requests that work well

- "Build a fact sheet for our tutoring program from the numbers below. Every figure has its date and source."
- "Here are the foundation's guidelines. Check our fit, then draft the program description in 500 words."
- "Write the budget justification for this budget table. The funder caps indirect costs at 10%."
- "Write a one-page impact update for donors for last year. Here are our numbers and one story we have consent to share."
- "Draft a thank-you email for a tribute gift in memory of `[honoree name]`."
- "Plan a Saturday park cleanup for about 60 volunteers, with a run-of-show and a shift schedule."
- "Write a role description for a weekend food pantry volunteer. Our policy requires references, no background check."

## Bigger jobs

Ask for the `npo-grants-agent` workflow, or say: "Help me apply to this foundation, from our fact sheet to the budget narrative." The agent works through the steps and stops after each one for you to approve or change it.

## Check everything before you submit or send

1. Every number, outcome, and quote is real, sourced, and current. Search for `[number from your records]` and fill in or remove each one.
2. It follows the funder's guidelines, limits, and any AI-use policy.
3. No donor, volunteer, or client personal details were entered, or your setup is approved for them.
4. Donor preferences, such as anonymity and how they like to be contacted, are honored.
5. Stories are used with consent and told with dignity.
6. Receipt and tax wording, budgets, and safeguarding steps were checked by someone qualified.
